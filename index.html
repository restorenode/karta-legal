Karta: privacy and terms (pilot)

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Privacy

Karta is a flashcard app for learning languages. This version is a pilot for a class: a teacher gives students a class code, and the code adds the class's cards to the app.

What Karta collects: nothing. There is no account and no sign-in. Karta does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and it shows no ads.

What stays on your phone. Your study progress (which cards you know, your daily goal and streak), your settings (language, theme) and the class code you entered are stored only on your phone, in an encrypted database. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them, and they cannot be recovered.

No tracking. Karta has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it does not share data with third parties.

Audio. The words and sentences you hear were recorded in advance and are built into the app. Playing them sends nothing over the internet.

TestFlight. If you installed Karta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports, usage information and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send, and shares them with the developer to fix problems. This is covered by Apple's own privacy policy and TestFlight terms.

Children. Karta collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Contact. Questions: kartalearn.support@gmail.com.

Terms of use

  • Karta is provided for learning, free of charge, as a pilot. It may change, and it may stop being available.
  • It is provided "as is", without warranties. We try to keep the cards correct, but they may contain mistakes; your teacher's materials come first.
  • Use it for your own study. Don't copy the app's content for other purposes or try to break the app.
  • Class codes are for the students of that class. Please don't share them publicly.

Karta：隱私權與條款（試用版）

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 19 日

隱私權

Karta 是一款學習語言的單字卡 App。這個版本是給課堂使用的試用版：老師提供班級代碼，輸入代碼後，App 會加入該班的單字卡。

Karta 不收集任何資料。 不需要帳號，也不需要登入。Karta 不會要求你的姓名、電子郵件、電話號碼、位置、聯絡人或照片，也沒有廣告。

資料只存在你的手機裡。 你的學習進度（已掌握的卡片、每日目標、連續學習天數）、設定（語言、主題）以及你輸入的班級代碼，只會加密儲存在你的手機上，不會傳送給我們或任何人。刪除 App 會一併刪除這些資料，且無法復原。

不追蹤。 Karta 沒有使用分析、廣告或追蹤工具，也不會與第三方分享資料。

語音。 你聽到的單字和句子都是事先錄好、內建在 App 裡的，播放時不會透過網路傳送任何資料。

TestFlight。 如果你是透過 Apple 的 TestFlight 安裝 Karta，Apple 可能會收集當機報告、使用資訊，以及你選擇送出的意見回饋或截圖，並提供給開發者以修正問題。這部分適用 Apple 的隱私權政策與 TestFlight 條款。

兒童。 Karta 不會向任何人（包括兒童）收集個人資料。

變更。 若本政策有所變更，新版本會公布在此網址，並更新日期。

聯絡方式。 如有問題，請聯絡：kartalearn.support@gmail.com。

使用條款

  • Karta 以試用版形式免費提供，用於學習。內容可能變更，也可能停止提供。
  • 本 App 依「現況」提供，不作任何保證。我們會盡力確保卡片內容正確，但仍可能有錯誤；請以老師的教材為準。
  • 請用於個人學習。請勿將 App 內容複製作其他用途，或試圖破壞 App。
